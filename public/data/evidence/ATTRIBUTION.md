# SiteCommit evidence attribution

## iVISION

Source: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/danielmao2019/ivision-fall2024

Credit: Dayou Mao, Yuchen Lin, Ashkan Ebadi, John Zelek, Alexander Wong and Yuhao Chen; iVISION research contributors.

The dataset publisher identifies the release as MIT. License text is retained at `../../licenses/IVISION-MIT.txt`; fresh publisher metadata is in `pipeline/ivision-license-2026-10-03.json` in the source repository. Forty-eight original photographs were resized to WebP, four on each of twelve actual capture dates. Exact paths and hashes are in `catalog.json`.

## Montijo

“Construction site timelapse photos from Montijo in Portugal”, Rui Barros Garcia and Ruben Pereira Silva, Garcia, Garcia S.A.

Source: https://zenodo.org/records/21055820

License: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Twelve original photographs were selected from the published archive and resized to WebP. No construction content was generated or replaced. Exact filenames, original hashes, modifications and source information are in `montijo.json`. Image filename dates are used because the source description has an inconsistent date range. Framing changes between periods; these are not registered multi-view scans.

## Added review material

Plans, example drawings and seed annotations were created for this application, not supplied by either site owner. Every example is labeled “Illustrative example, created for this demo”. Later notes and dimensions are reviewer-entered. Gaussian models belong only to the separate three-date iVISION survey experience. Neither dataset supplies validated quantities, approved bills or contractual measurement records.
