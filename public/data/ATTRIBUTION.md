The source SOG data and source preview are mirrored without geometric edits. The demo rotates the scene for display and applies **illustrative vertical visibility masks** at three heights. The phase stills are rendered from those masked views. Phase labels, week numbers, callouts, and annotations were added for this demo. They are NOT recorded capture dates, actual historical construction stages, or automatically detected changes. No new physical geometry is generated. The complete visible capture is restored at the last phase.
